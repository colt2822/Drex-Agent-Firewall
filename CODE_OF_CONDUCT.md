# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, with project-specific reporting details below.

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity. We aim to maintain a welcoming, harassment-free environment for contributors and community members.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Share feedback that focuses on the work and helps improve it.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct their impact.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement and Reporting

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the conduct. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact options on their GitHub profiles. Do not include live credentials or unnecessary personal information in a report.
